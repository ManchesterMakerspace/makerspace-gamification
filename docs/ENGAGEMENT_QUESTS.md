# Progress, reviewed quests, and The System

## Member interfaces

`/ledger stats` opens your character sheet with authoritative rank, XP, deepest cleared skill, shop milestones, mentoring, volunteer contributions, and achievements. Before metrics are available, it shows a pending history-import message or an explicit empty state. **Next rank**, **Browse quests**, **Skill tree**, **Achievements**, and **Preferences** match the Home controls. `/ledger progress` shows cumulative requirements from your pinned rules, deficits, remaining XP, and up to three optional next steps. Pending history imports, staff holds, membership eligibility, and the highest configured rank are shown privately. The Ledger explains facts; it cannot promise promotion.

Ask about your progress in a DM, mention, active Ledger thread, or a clear self-directed question in a registered Ledger channel. Replies stay in context and include a private detail button. Membership eligibility details stay private.

`/ledger-quests` or `/ledger-quests list` opens a searchable title dropdown. Details show readable description, observable criteria, exact legacy target or new minimum rank, duration, prerequisites, optional example photo, approved reward, creator attribution, verification, and actions. Existing learning challenges and cooperative quests remain available through the same browser. `/ledger-skills` remains the complete skill tree.

Every active participant in good standing may use `/ledger-quests create` for an individual or cooperative proposal. Choose an enabled minimum rank at or below your own, duration, and up to 20 visible, enabled, in-service tools for which you currently hold non-revoked checkouts; parent shops must also be enabled and in service. Cooperative proposals need two to four disciplines. One optional JPEG/PNG/GIF example photo may be attached up to 10 MiB. **Help me draft** and **Rewrite for clarity** produce retry-stable editable prose suggestions in the background; you explicitly submit. The System cannot publish a quest or change authoritative fields.

Reviewers may edit the proposal but must remain within the proposer's current eligible tools and their own authority over every original and edited shop. Approval rechecks all state, preserves the original, and publishes an immutable reviewed child when edited. The reviewer sets completion XP and a one-time proposer bonus, both whole numbers from 0–500 XP and both defaulting to 100. New quests accept participants at or above the minimum rank with every live prerequisite; legacy published quests retain exact-rank rules. Accepted revision and reward survive later promotion.

Proposers may complete or contribute to their own quests but cannot publish, review, verify, or finalize their work. They earn 5% of XP actually credited for each other member's verified individual completion, rounded half-up, or one 5% share of cooperative group XP excluding themselves. First positive share gets a New Achievement message; subsequent positive individual shares get an ordinary private System DM. A rounded-zero share has no record or message. `/ledger-quests edit <revision>` submits a fresh immutable revision for review.

If completion evidence is rejected, resubmit the corrected details. Every submission attempt and review has its own version-specific ID, retaining the original description, rejection reason, reviewer and outcome. Specialized milestones also save new underlying evidence with corrected learners, mentor and handoff. Mentoring resubmissions require fresh acknowledgment from the listed learners. Repeated submission while review is pending reuses the saved attempt and sends no additional acknowledgment requests. The reviewer approves that attempt and the once-only quest reward together. Existing legacy records are preserved when new attempts are created or pending legacy attempts are reviewed.

Rank corrections, suspension, revocation, or invalid identity can disable authored quests and block outstanding awards. Completed history remains. Restoration requires a new reviewed revision; disabled accepted revisions remain unavailable. Author opt-out retains reviewed published quests but stops authoring and game notifications.

## Preferences and recognition

Observation defaults on for every eligible linked human member in configured Ledger channels, including members who have never joined or have left the game. Use `/ledger preferences`, uncheck **Allow observation**, and save to opt out without joining or leaving. Game participation and arrival mentions are separate choices. Saved observation opt-outs survive updates, first join and rejoin. An explanatory notice must be delivered before capture begins; a nonparticipant's first eligible channel message queues that notice and is excluded from observation. The System observes only new eligible messages in registered Ledger channels, kudos issuance metadata, and verified volunteer activity. It excludes DMs, original kudos text, unrelated channels, bots, historical imports, and replays.

Observation suggestions are always audit-only, including when `LEDGER_OBSERVATION_AUDIT_ONLY=false`. Python records the proposed category and XP as `proposed_delta`, with applied `delta: 0`. Suggestions do not change XP or rank, consume budgets, send warnings or recognition messages, or publish achievements. A separate authorized deterministic or human decision is required before changing accounting; this runtime has no automatic proposal approval path. Regular earned XP and independently reviewed quest rewards continue normally.

Before delayed observation evaluation, Python validates each record independently. Edited/deleted messages, invalid kudos attribution and revoked volunteer evidence are cancelled with a reason and excluded from inference. Valid records still proceed in batches of up to eight; excess valid records receive a follow-up job. A batch containing only invalid records finishes without inference, allowing later activity to proceed.

Existing finalized discretionary records, historical budget consumption and authorized append-only staff corrections are retained. Audit validation still bounds suggestions and requires evidence/consent, including delivered-warning causality for repeat-imitation proposals. Ordinary gratitude or similar wording is insufficient.

Arrival mentions are optional. A fresh canonical check-in has a reserved 20% default chance of a greeting, with a durable ten-day cooldown across rank changes. Greetings use only your current available rank channel and respect voluntary departures. The System receives no card identifier. An uncertain Slack send retains the cooldown.

## Operator review and authority

`/ledger-admin delegates` lets current staff select one opted-in eligible member, explicit capabilities, scope, and a required reason. Admins/board members can grant global, explicit-shop, or quest scopes. Resource managers can grant only within their current assigned shops. All relevant shops must be covered; unscoped evidence requires global authority unless a specific quest grant applies. No self-grants or onward delegation.

For quest scope, enter the ID of the original quest or any revision. The application stores the stable logical quest ID so publication and completion grants apply across that quest's revisions; cooperative group quests keep their own ID. The selected revision remains the shop-eligibility reference, and each review's actual shops must still be within the grantor's current authority. Existing revision-scoped grants resolve through their quest without rewriting scope, grant version or audit history; revoked grants remain revoked.

Capabilities are `quest_publish`, `quest_complete`, `learning_review`, and `mentoring_review`. Rank and descriptive titles grant nothing. `/ledger-admin review` filters the queue by actual authorization. `/ledger-admin publish-quest <revision>` and `reject-quest <revision>` open publication/reward forms. `approve <evidence-id>` and `reject <evidence-id> <reason>` review pending evidence and quest completions. Existing `verify-quest <quest> @member` verifies cooperative contributions; contributors cannot verify their group. Authors can `/ledger-quests withdraw <revision>`; staff with the complete scope can `/ledger-admin disable-quest <revision> <reason>`. Admins/board members can `/ledger-admin correct-ai <decision-id> <signed-XP-delta> <reason>` for an independent append-only correction linked to the original decision, without replenishing budgets.

Delegates cannot configure ranks/prompts, attest membership, grant tool clearance, issue arbitrary XP, reverse finalized approvals, or delegate. Python rechecks independence, current participation/membership/identity, grant capability/scope/version, and grantor authority immediately before a review commit. Audits retain grant ID/version. Explicit revocation writes the same grant document as review use, serializing with approval. Opt-out revokes all received grants in the consent transaction. Source revocation events and reconciliation permanently revoke grants; live checks deny them before cleanup. Restoring membership or rejoining never revives grants. Legitimate past approvals remain; staff corrections are separate.

## Technical implementation and rollout

All application-authored member-facing text uses **The Ledger** or **The System**. Implementation model names remain confined to technical configuration and operator documentation. Member-authored kudos and other supplied content are preserved.

No new collections: grants/acceptances and nonparticipant preference profiles use `ledger_relationships`; immutable quests use `ledger_quests`; observations, decisions, daily budgets, warnings, audits, and cooldowns use `ledger_evidence`; awards use `ledger_awards`; jobs and context keep their existing collections. `_id` serializes daily workspace/member budgets and cooldown reservations. Additional indexes cover quest targets/creators, grant delegate/grantor/scope/status, acceptances, and evidence review/day queries.

Source reads use the official PyMongo client through `MLAB_URI`; owned writes use `LEDGER_URI`. Query tools expose only `shops`, `tools`, `tool_checkouts`, `volunteer_tasks`, and `volunteer_events`, fixed fields, escaped literal search, caller-only clearances, mandatory enabled-parent filters, Rails claimable task statuses, and New York date-only event/cooldown boundaries. Internal `checkins`/`cards` reads are exclusively for canonical arrival identity. MQTT subscribes explicitly to `checkins/insert` independently of query permissions, and excludes retained packets. Review [exact Mongo roles](MONGODB_ACCESS.md) before rollout.

The narration transport rejects tool calls. The separate conversation transport validates every tool request and correlates results with `tool_call_id`, at most three calls in thirty seconds. Query reads use a combined two-second server-query budget and bound matching results after enabled-parent/caller filters; a large unrelated catalog no longer fails a narrow query. Tool unavailable results are distinct from empty results. Skill labels/topology may use sanitized display metadata no older than five minutes, with live safety/prerequisite/clearance checks; progress and deepest-current-skill facts stay live. [Interactive-read rollout](QUERY_OPTIMIZATION.md) covers preparation and rollback. [Slack external selects](https://docs.slack.dev/reference/block-kit/block-elements/select-menu-element/), [hash-protected view updates](https://docs.slack.dev/reference/methods/views.update/), [function calling](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/function-calling), and [PyMongo projections](https://www.mongodb.com/docs/languages/python/pymongo-driver/current/crud/query/project/) document the transport contracts.

Switches are independent:

| Environment setting | Default | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `LEDGER_OBSERVATION` | `true` | Capture/evaluate new eligible observations after notices, unless a member opts out |
| `LEDGER_OBSERVATION_AUDIT_ONLY` | `true` | Compatibility setting; proposals remain audit-only even when false |
| `LEDGER_DEDUCTIONS` | `false` | Permit validation of repeat-imitation audit suggestions; cannot apply deductions |
| `LEDGER_NOVEL_ANNOUNCEMENTS` | `false` | Legacy finalized-notification delivery gate; new proposals never publish |
| `LEDGER_WELCOMES` | `false` | Permit reserved arrival greetings |
| `LEDGER_WELCOME_PROBABILITY` | `0.2` | Once-per-arrival selection probability |
| `LEDGER_OPTIMIZED_READS` | `true` in Python; `false` in the example | Enable prepared aggregate/batched/display-cache reads |
| `LEDGER_QUERY_METRICS` | `false` | Aggregate Mongo counters without filters or bodies |

Observation is enabled by default in Python, Compose and `.env.example`. An existing explicit `LEDGER_OBSERVATION=false` remains a deployment-wide disable switch; remove that override or set it to `true`, then rebuild/recreate services to use the new default. For staged acceptance checks, set it to `false` until live verification completes. Run `ledger init` to add indexes and update the least-privilege source role. Verify real Mongo permissions, Slack external-select options, modal hashes, approval/revocation conflicts on the replica set, and bot membership. Inspect observation proposals/rejections as audit evidence. Automatic recognition, deductions and proposal announcements cannot be enabled by flags. Configure welcomes independently. Cancelled/failed explanatory notices are requeued for the current generation of an eligible identity after re-enablement; their deterministic ID is retained, retry attempts reset and the old lease removed. Pending/working/done notices and observation-opted-out/older generations are not reopened. Encountered nonparticipants have notices retried by periodic reconciliation. Leaving the game does not disable observation; use Preferences for that choice.

`ledger-engagement` consumes only engagement inbox jobs. Accounting excludes those jobs; channel cleanup has its own worker. Accounting, delegation revocation, opt-out, and channel removal do not depend on inference or Google Docs. `/ledger-admin metrics` exposes backlog, conversation latency, engagement outcomes, budget rejections, deductions, delegation revocations/failures, arrival outcomes, and delivery statuses without bodies or private source records.

Compose adds `--enable-auto-tool-choice --tool-call-parser qwen3_coder` and preserves non-thinking generation. The [configured NVIDIA checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/nvidia/Qwen3.8-27B-NVFP4) recommends this parser. **Deployed-image compatibility remains a live verification step:** check the image's parser support, startup, authenticated tool round-trip and normal narration before rolling out default observation. No live model, Slack, Mongo, or Google Doc verification is implied by local tests.

## Chat and peer thanks without joining

You may send and receive `/kudos` and chat with The Ledger without opting in. Pick an optional emoji in the sending modal; it accompanies the recipient DM and optional public post. Your original text stays unchanged. Joining controls kudos XP and game access, with the same repeat-giving limits.

Before joining, The Ledger answers general questions about itself and XP and may suggest `/ledger join` to see behind the curtain or start your journey. Specific ranks, quests and rules become available after joining. Participant conversations cover current/lower rank names and details and what remains for the next promotion, without revealing other higher-rank details or quests beyond your rank. In joined channels, address The Ledger by name or mention or continue one of its threads. Only registered Ledger channels examine unaddressed questions containing `?` and self-directed progress requests. Unrelated ambient conversations in other channels are excluded from inference and chat context. Shop/tool answers use available catalog facts, knowledge and relevant conversation history; unknown information receives "I don't know." Chat never enrolls a member in the game. Observation in configured channels is independently enabled by default after notice and can be disabled through Preferences.

## Review-channel notifications

Set `LEDGER_QUEST_REVIEW_CHANNEL_ID` to a private, non-external staff channel and invite The Ledger bot. It must be separate from registered member game channels. Limit channel membership to people authorized to see the full review queue; membership itself grants no approval authority. Blank disables new general review notification reservations; already targeted jobs retain their saved destination for retries. Compose passes the setting to every application worker.

Quest publication proposals (member or Ledger authored), individual completion attempts, learning/mentoring evidence and group contributions queue durable `review_notice` outbox jobs in the same owned-data transaction. Specialized evidence linked to a quest uses the top-level completion message. When all required cooperative contributions are verified, the shared project also queues its completion review. No inference is used for review notices, and approvals still enforce existing staff/delegate scope, independent review and evidence gates in Python.

The activity stores `review_message_ts` and `review_channel_id`; group contributions store these fields inside their contribution record. Acknowledgments, approvals, rejections, publication, supersession, withdrawal, disablement and shared-project closure refresh the same message. Each immutable resubmission/revision has its own address. Delivery reloads current activity state, so a review closed before its first post appears closed. `chat.update` returning `message_not_found` triggers a replacement post and saves its new timestamp; other Slack errors retry without a duplicate post. Changing the configured channel posts a new message there and saves that address.

Per-activity leases serialize concurrent deliveries; saved post reservations keep client message IDs stable across uncertain retries. Receipt writes preserve concurrent review decisions. Periodic reconciliation backfills existing pending work and retries failed/cancelled notification jobs after configuration is repaired. Slack failures do not roll back reviews or accounting. Existing `chat:write` supports both posting and updating; no new scope, collection or source write is required. Live Slack and Mongo replica-set behavior still need deployment verification.

## Ledger-authored quest proposals

See [quest generation](QUEST_GENERATION.md) for the standalone CLI, weighted rank selection, historical channel inspiration, immutable review edits, ordinary challenge rewards and independent shared-project finalization. Generation/submission creates no XP or published availability. Historical inspiration is a separate purpose from audit-only observation; publish its channel-use explanation before enabling operators.

New eligible kudos metadata and verified volunteer activity also trigger explanatory observation notices, including for nonparticipants. The first activity is excluded until notice delivery; original kudos bodies remain outside observation. A Slack-confirmed notice receipt is saved for the same consent generation even if processing is paused or observation/eligibility/preferences change during posting. Live eligibility continues to gate capture and inference. Legacy done notice jobs missing their receipt can be reopened on reconciliation after eligibility resumes.
